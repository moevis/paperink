# Formula Typesetting Test

Inline: $a^{1/n}$, $x^{1/2}$, $2^{-3}$

## Simple fractions

$$\frac{1}{n}$$

$$\frac{a}{b}$$

$$\frac{m+n}{2}$$

## A fraction inside a superscript

$$a^{\frac{1}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a}$$

## Nesting

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

$$\frac{\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}}{2}$$

## Radicals

$$\sqrt{2} \quad \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \quad \sqrt[n]{a^m}$$
